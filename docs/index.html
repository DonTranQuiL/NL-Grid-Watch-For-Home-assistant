Dutch grid-stress forecast for Home Assistant

Backfeed by day.
Evening peak by night.

Turns your postcode into a local overload forecast, using the Netbeheer Nederland capaciteitskaart and Open-Meteo weather. Think of it like a weather forecast, not a promise about cable faults.

What you get

Two directions on the same cable. High means that windows like this are when areas like yours see inverter trips or local overload.

Backfeed risk

Teruglevering already tight and the next midday (10:00–16:00) is clear. The usual result is an inverter trip; a real outage is the failure case.

Evening-peak risk

Afname already tight and the coming evening (16:00–22:00) is dark, cloudy or cold. Think cooking, heat pumps and EV chargers.

Official capacity map

Netbeheer Nederland capaciteitskaart colours for afname and teruglevering, plus the grid operator, supply area and queue size.

Just your postcode

No account or API key. PDOK turns the postcode into a location. Add one entry per postcode you care about.

Last-good data

A failed poll keeps the last snapshot on screen, and diagnostic sensors show poll health.

Blueprint and interruptions

A one-click notification blueprint, plus optional energieonderbrekingen.nl outages if you have credentials.

How the forecast works

Area status from the capacity map, combined with the Open-Meteo hourly forecast for today and tomorrow.

Backfeed (10:00–16:00)

  • High: teruglevering is investigation or queue, radiation ≥ 500 W/m² and clouds ≤ 40%
  • Low: teruglevering is limited or worse, radiation ≥ 300 W/m²

Evening peak (16:00–22:00)

  • High: afname is investigation or queue, and the evening has clouds ≥ 70%, ≤ 8 °C, or falls in November–February
  • Low: afname is limited with the same evening conditions
available limited investigation queue

The capaciteitskaart covers connections above 3×80 A: it describes your area, not your street cabinet.

Entities you get

One device per postcode (NL Grid Watch 1012AB). Risk windows (start, end, hours, peak radiation, minimum temperature) are in the attributes.

sensor.…_afname_status
area status (draw)
sensor.…_teruglevering_status
area status (feed-in)
sensor.…_backfeed_risk
none · low · high
sensor.…_evening_peak_risk
none · low · high
binary_sensor.…_stress_expected
on when a risk is high
sensor.…_interruption
optional outages

Plus diagnostic sensors (consecutive errors, last update status and time) and the nl_grid_watch.refresh service.

Act on it

Pause EV charging during an evening peak, or switch on the boiler to use your own solar power when backfeed is likely. Or just import the blueprint and get a notification.

Import the blueprint 
triggers:
  - trigger: state
    entity_id: sensor.nl_grid_watch_1012ab_evening_peak_risk
    to: "high"
actions:
  - action: switch.turn_off
    target:
      entity_id: switch.ev_charger

Installation

HACS, a restart, and your postcode.

HACS

  1. 1. Click Open in HACS, or add https://github.com/DonTranQuiL/NL-Grid-Watch-For-Home-assistant as a custom repository (category Integration).
  2. 2. Download NL Grid Watch and restart Home Assistant.
  3. 3. Settings → Devices & services → Add integration → NL Grid Watch, then enter your postcode.

Optional: interruptions

Have energieonderbrekingen.nl client credentials? Enter them during setup or under Configure to get planned and active outages. The forecast doesn't need them.

Full docs, dashboard YAML and troubleshooting are in the README.
